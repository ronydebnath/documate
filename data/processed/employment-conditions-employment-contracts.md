An employment contract is an agreement between an employer and employee that sets out terms and conditions of employment.

Find more information on employment contracts, including what advice we can give on them.

## In this section...

### About employment contracts

Understand employment contracts and what rules they set out.

### Get help with employment contracts

We can give advice about minimum workplace entitlements in employment contracts. If we can’t answer a question, we will recommend you seek legal help.

### Make an employment contract

Employers can use a free business.gov.au tool to build an employment contract that’s tailored to their business needs and complies with workplace laws.
